Recovery & Fueling

The training gets you the stimulus. What you do in the hours after is what lets you show up again in two days instead of five.

Protein window
30–40g

Within about 60–90 minutes post-session. Muscle protein synthesis slows with age, so this window matters more at 40+ than it did at 25.

Hydration
½–¾ oz / lb bodyweight

Daily baseline, more on high-sweat conditioning days. Add electrolytes on anything over 40 minutes of sustained work.

Joint care
Move before you load

Ten minutes of controlled range-of-motion work before high joint-load sessions does more for longevity than any supplement.

Sleep
7–9 hrs

The main recovery driver you actually control. Strength adaptation and connective tissue repair both lean on it heavily.
